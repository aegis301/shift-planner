# Brainstorm

- LLM assistant that can answer "who is free next Friday?" from MCP resources.
- German email draft generator for monthly wishes and no-gos.
- Constraint explanation panel that translates rule warnings into plain language.
- Solver mode that proposes several roster variants with fairness scores.

- Configurable shift-slot templates, e.g. two day doctors on weekdays and one night doctor every day.
- Side-by-side conflict overlay that shows wishes while editing the final roster matrix.
